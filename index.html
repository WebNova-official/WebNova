FAQ

Questions? We've got
clear answers.

Here are the most common questions we get from new clients.

How long does it take to build a website?

+

Most websites are delivered within 3–7 business days. Simple landing pages can be done in 2–3 days. Complex web apps or e-commerce stores may take 2–4 weeks depending on features. We give you a clear timeline upfront before starting any project.

Do I need to provide content like text and images?

+

Ideally yes — your own content will always perform better. However, if you don't have content ready, our team can help write copy and source relevant images as part of your package. Just let us know and we'll factor that into the plan.

What is a Google Business Profile and why do I need it?

+

A Google Business Profile (GBP) is a free listing that shows your business on Google Maps and local search results. When someone searches "[your service] in [your city]", a well-optimised GBP puts you in front of those customers. It's one of the highest ROI investments for any local business.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept UPI, bank transfer, and all major payment apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm). Typically we ask for 50% advance to begin work and the remaining 50% on delivery. For larger projects we can arrange milestone-based payments.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

+

Yes! We either build with a simple CMS so you can make updates yourself, or we're available for quick edits via WhatsApp. For clients on monthly social media or GBP packages, content updates are included.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

+

Absolutely — we work with clients all across India and internationally. Everything is handled remotely via WhatsApp, email, and video calls. Our clients are based in Pune, Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi, and beyond.
